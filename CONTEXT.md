# Via Nova Marketing Website

This context describes the public-facing language for Via Nova's marketing site,
especially how the program, participants, and formation offerings are named.

## Language

**Via**: A holistic formation program for young adults centered on study, work,
prayer, and communal life. _Avoid_: Gap year program as the only description

**Via Fellow**: A young adult participant in Via. _Avoid_: Student, participant,
resident when Fellow is meant

**Core curriculum**: The required syllabus of seminars that all Via Fellows
study. _Avoid_: Main classes, required courses

**Optional semester-long course**: An additional course that Via Fellows may
choose to take alongside the core curriculum, with topics changing year by year.
_Avoid_: Supplemental course, other course

## Relationships

- A **Via Fellow** participates in **Via**.
- A **Via Fellow** studies the **core curriculum**.
- A **Via Fellow** may participate in zero or more **optional semester-long
  courses**.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Should the FAQ say Fellows take supplemental courses?" **Domain
> expert:** "Call them **optional semester-long courses**. They are in addition
> to the **core curriculum**, and their content varies year by year."

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Other courses" and "supplemental courses" were used for the same offering —
  resolved: use **optional semester-long courses**.
